Frequently asked questions

Are the listed prices final?

The website prices are starting estimates. The final price is confirmed after photos or an inspection, depending on the item and its condition.

Can I get a quote from photos?

Yes. Send clear photos of the item or area on WhatsApp, along with your suburb and what you need cleaned.

Do you travel outside Retreat?

Spotless Solutions is based in Retreat, Cape Town. Add your suburb to your request so the team can confirm availability and any travel costs.

How do I secure a booking?

Send your request first. The team will confirm the date, final amount and deposit details with you.
